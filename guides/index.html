Park guides for dog owners

Every park has its own rules, and most of them are confusing. These guides say where your dog can go, where they cannot, and what to do about it. One park per page, written for a phone.

The short version of park rules

  • National parks: dogs stay on paved roads, in campgrounds and picnic areas. Trails are almost always off limits.
  • California state parks: the same by default, but each park sets its own exceptions. Some open beaches and fire roads; some ban dogs entirely.
  • Everywhere: a leash of 6 feet or less, never leave the dog alone, and pick up after them.
  • The paw score on each card rates how much of the park your dog can actually enjoy, from 1 (banned) to 5 (most trails and beaches).
